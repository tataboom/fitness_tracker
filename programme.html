A clearer plan. A better rep.

The principles behind your home-gym companion.

Your four-day foundation

The default is an upper/lower split on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, for general fitness. New or returning lifters begin with two working sets per movement. Choose your days, experience and session length in Settings. Existing workouts keep their original exercises when you change your plan.

These are starter templates and transparent progression rules, not a personal assessment or a live trainer. If you are learning to lift, a qualified coach can check your technique and adapt exercises to you.

Warm up, practise, then log

Begin with easy movement and lighter rehearsal sets. Read the movement guide before unfamiliar exercises. Work through a comfortable range with control, breathe throughout, and finish with a few clean reps left. Stop movements that cause pain.

Tap a set’s checkmark only after you complete it. A planned weight or an unfinished input is not a completed lift. The rest timer starts after the checkmark. You can correct a set by tapping its checkmark again.

Progress from your own baseline

Build reps within the suggested range. The app considers a weight increase only after every set is completed at a consistent load, every set reaches the top of the range, and you record at least two reps left on every set. It respects configured equipment limits and holds progression after reported pain or a demanding session.

A recommendation is an option, not a requirement. Timed holds do not receive kilogram increases. Bodyweight movements progress through control and appropriate variations.

Your actual equipment

The linked Domyos set lists 80 kg of plates, an 8.7 kg barbell and two 2 kg dumbbell handles. The app therefore starts with an 88.7 kg barbell inventory limit. Check sleeve space and the equipment instructions; not every theoretical plate combination will fit.

Use the supplied collars. The listing specifies a 15 kg vertical-grip limit and says not to use these dumbbells for overhead exercises. Those exercises are excluded from new plans.

The Rebel Active listing gives a 25 kg attachment limit and a 220 kg bench capacity. Verify exactly what the bench rating includes in the supplied manual. Barbell stands have not been confirmed as safety catches: the app requires rated safety arms to include solo barbell bench pressing. Do not rely on removing collars or tipping plates as a safety method.

Look for trends, not verdicts

Follow completed sessions, sets and strength trends. Check your body weight under similar conditions; weekly averages add context to daily variation. Waist measurements can be useful alongside weight, but neither measurement can identify muscle gain or fat loss directly.

The coach report includes effort, skipped exercises, discomfort and your notes. It can help a trainer understand more than the weight on the bar.

Further technique guidance

The illustrations in this app are original, simplified movement diagrams. Use their setup, movement and return cues together. They cannot assess your posture in real time.
